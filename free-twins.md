# Free twins

Open-source tools that replace something you're probably paying for. Each list matches a reel from [@ishaan.clearlyy](https://www.instagram.com/ishaan.clearlyy/).

Star counts are from the date shown. "Free" means free to run yourself: you supply the computer, and sometimes the AI model.

## Stop paying for AI tools · 12 Nov 2026

| Instead of | Try | Stars | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | [Jan](https://github.com/janhq/jan) | 45.7K | Runs offline, so it's only as smart as the model your laptop can handle. |
| Claude Code | [OpenCode](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode) | 226K | You still bring a model, either through an API key or a local model. |
| Wispr Flow | [OpenWhispr](https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr) | 11.4K | Local models need a decent machine for fast dictation. |
| Zapier | [n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n) | 210K | Fair-code license, so read it before you build a business on it. |
| Midjourney | [ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) | 146K | Needs a good GPU, and there's a learning curve. |

## Stop paying for creator tools · 12 Nov 2026

| Instead of | Try | Stars | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adobe Acrobat | [Stirling-PDF](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF) | 96.4K | You host it yourself, through Docker or the desktop app. |
| Topaz Photo AI | [Upscayl](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl) | 52.3K | Upscaling only. It doesn't do Topaz's other tools. |
| Google Photos | [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich) | 119K | Needs a server or a NAS that you keep backed up. |
| Notion | [AppFlowy](https://github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy) | 78.8K | Fewer integrations than Notion. |
| Figma | [Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot) | 62.3K | Smaller plugin ecosystem. |

## Stop paying for builder tools · 12 Nov 2026

| Instead of | Try | Stars | The catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Postman | [Hoppscotch](https://github.com/hoppscotch/hoppscotch) | 80.8K | Some team features need their cloud. |
| Firebase | [Supabase](https://github.com/supabase/supabase) | 115K | Self-hosting it is real work. Their cloud has a free tier. |
| Google Analytics | [Plausible](https://github.com/plausible/analytics) | 29.8K | Simpler reports, by design. |
| Salesforce | [Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty) | 60.8K | Young product, so check it has the features you need. |
| Calendly | [Cal.diy](https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy) | 50K | You host it yourself, or use their hosted plan. |

## Run AI on your own laptop · 12 Nov 2026

| What | Repo | Stars | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run models locally | [Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama) | 187K | The engine most of the others plug into |
| ChatGPT-style interface | [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui) | 158K | A chat window for Ollama models |
| Chat with your documents | [AnythingLLM](https://github.com/Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm) | 68.2K | PDFs, notes and folders |
| Offline desktop chat | [Jan](https://github.com/janhq/jan) | 45.7K | One app, nothing to set up |
| Images and video | [ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) | 146K | Node-based image and video generation |

Before installing anything, run the [60-second repo check](SAFETY.md).
